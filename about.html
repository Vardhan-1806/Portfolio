I'm Alisam Srivardhan, a computer science student and software engineer based in Hyderabad. My work is anchored at the intersection of technical rigor, algorithmic problem solving, and aesthetic precision.

Whether I am implementing a RAG-based AI legal assistant with sub-2-second retrieval times, crafting responsive interfaces with pixel-level care, or tackling medium and hard graph problems on LeetCode, I approach software with the patient observation of a draftsman and the progressive overload principle of a lifter.

Art teaches observation. Long before writing code, I filled sketchbooks with pencil portraits. Drawing trains you to observe negative space, balance, and proportions rather than rushing to make assumptions. In engineering, that same skill means seeing edge cases in an algorithm, identifying UI hierarchy flaws, and designing systems where every component serves an intentional purpose.

Fitness teaches consistency. A 50-day streak on LeetCode or a complex full-stack deployment does not happen through bursts of motivation; it happens through systems and habits. The discipline built in the gym carries straight into debugging race conditions and shipping clean, maintainable code.
